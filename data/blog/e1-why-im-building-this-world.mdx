> **Experiencing fictional worlds is one way humans make sense of reality.**

I've always loved fictional worlds. They offer something special: the chance to step outside the ordinary and breathe somewhere else. There, values, conflicts, and possibilities can be pushed further than everyday life allows.

One may think it's a form of escape from reality. It's not. It's an expansion of it.

Fiction lets us simulate experiences safely. We practice understanding other minds, exploring consequences, testing values, without real-world risk. [Research shows](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/between-cultures/202201/the-psychology-fiction-why-reading-transforms-us) that simulation improves how we navigate actual reality.

That explains the pull for me. But at some point, just visiting wasn't enough.

# From Player to Builder

For years, between work (I consult on tech innovation strategy), enjoying worlds meant playing in other people's settings: video games, books, films. Then, two years ago, I became a Dungeon Master. Yes, that kind of nerd. And something clicked.

Running a world is different from visiting one, you're forced to think about logic: why things work the way they do, how they connect, what happens when players push against the edges. It's discovery through creation. And when you have to make something coherent, you learn in a way that just experiencing stories never teaches.

That combination, creativity and systems thinking together, isn't new for me. I've spent years building frameworks for organizations, written songs and performed them, studied Chinese linguistics. Different fields, same instinct: shape a structure, make it work.

Building a world from scratch sounded like the logical next step.

# What's Different About This World

I don't know if this World will be good. But I know it will have my touch. I've been lucky to do many different things in 33 years. Graduate in Chinese language at SOAS London. Live in China. Work across the Middle East. Sit in rooms where worldviews collide.

All of that ends up here. A World where cosmology shapes language, and language shapes how mortals reach the divine. Where the words you speak determine which gods hear you.

I don't know what the World will become. A setting. A book. Something else. What I know is that the foundations have to hold first. The rest comes after.

# The Goal of This Website

**The Sacred Canvas is the World's home.**

Over time, it will contain the World's cosmology, history, languages, cultures, and conflicts, organized as a setting that can be explored, read, and eventually played in. But the World isn't presented as finished. As I build it, I also document the decisions behind it: why a system exists, why a rule was chosen, why something was rebuilt or discarded.

So what you'll find here is both:
- the World as it exists so far;
- the reasoning that shaped it.

There's no fixed endpoint yet. The World grows as its foundations are designed, tested, and locked into place.

# If You're Still Here

This isn't a product launch. It's a workshop with the door open. Why build in public? Partly accountability: if I write it down, I have to stick to it. Partly transparency: anyone can see how decisions were made. But mostly, sharing what I love is just part of who I am.
