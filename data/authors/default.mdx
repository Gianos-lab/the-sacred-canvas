I'm building Najik, a fantasy world from the ground up—cosmology, language, cultures—and showing how the pieces fit together as I go.

This isn't finished lore. It's a workshop. Each post documents a decision, the reasoning behind it, and what it produces.

The Sacred Canvas is where the world takes shape in public, one piece at a time.
