Hey! I'm Gian, and I'm building a fantasy world in public.

I'm Sicilian by birth: grew up on arancini, countryside, horses, sea. I'm moved by four main things:

- **Music:** I spent my adolescence studying guitar and piano, then toured Italy as a songwriter
- **Language:** At 19 I moved to Venice to study Chinese, then Kunming for immersion
- **Innovation:** MSc at SOAS London, then Accenture, where I've learned to work on complex stuff across geographies with people smarter than me
- **Worlds:** I love fictional worlds. In my free time, I'm a Reader, Gamer, and Dungeon Master.

I have a wonderful girlfriend, a daughter, and a cat who deeply hates me.

Here I'm working on my own fantasy setting, trying to blend all the things I've learned over 33 years. I'm building it in public showing how corporate rigor can combine with casual creativity.

Welcome to my workshop.
